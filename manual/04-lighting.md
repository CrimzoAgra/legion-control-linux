# Lighting

The **Lighting** page controls the keyboard, the front light bar and the back light (the rear vents).

## Choosing a zone

At the top, pick **All**, **Keyboard**, **Front light** or **Back light**. Every change after that applies to the
zone you picked, so each zone can have its own effect.

## Effects

- **Lenovo default**: your lighting from before you first used Legion Control
- **Static**: one colour
- **Breathing**: fades in and out
- **Rainbow wave**: colours flow across
- **Spiral rainbow**: a rainbow swirl
- **Colour change**: all lights change colour
- **Smooth**: a slow colour fade
- **Off**: no lighting

Static and Breathing let you pick a colour. The first colour in the list is your Caelestia accent colour.

**Brightness** applies to all the lights. The moving effects also have a **Speed** slider.

## Where your lighting is kept

Effects run in the keyboard itself and are saved into its lighting profile, so they stay after a restart, and on
Windows too. The first time you change anything, Legion Control backs up the lighting you had, and **Lenovo default**
puts it back.

The lid's "LEGION" lettering can't be changed from Linux yet; for now only Legion Space on Windows can.
