# Performance

The **Performance** page sets how much power the CPU and GPU may use. Higher modes are faster but louder and hotter.

## Power modes

Click a mode to switch to it:

- **Quiet**: silent, longest battery
- **Balanced**: everyday use
- **Performance**: gaming and heavy work
- **Extreme**: maximum power, loud fans
- **Custom**: your own power limits

You only see the modes your laptop's firmware offers.

## Custom tuning

Below the modes are sliders for the firmware's power and temperature limits, for example:

- CPU sustained power (PL1) and CPU boost power (PL2), and how long the boost lasts
- CPU power when the GPU is busy, and the CPU temperature limit
- GPU power (TGP), GPU Dynamic Boost, the GPU to CPU power shift, the GPU power offset on AC, and the GPU temperature
  limit

Choose **Custom** first; the sliders only change anything in that mode. A change applies when you let go of the
slider. Each slider stays within the range the firmware allows.
