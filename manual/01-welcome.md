# Welcome to Legion Control

Legion Control replaces Lenovo Legion Space and Vantage on Linux. It's one window with four pages, and it takes its
colours from your Caelestia theme.

## What you get

- **Performance**: power modes, and the firmware's power and temperature limits for the CPU and GPU (PL1, PL2, GPU TGP,
  Dynamic Boost and more). See [Performance](03-performance.md).
- **Lighting**: the keyboard, the front light bar and the back light (the rear vents), each with its own effect:
  static, breathing, rainbow wave, colour change, smooth, spiral, off, or your original lighting. Plus brightness. The
  effects are saved in the keyboard itself, so they stay after a restart and on Windows. See [Lighting](04-lighting.md).
- **Battery and keyboard**: conservation mode (a charge limit), USB charging while the laptop is off, and Fn lock. See
  [Battery and keyboard](05-battery-and-keyboard.md).
- **Updates**: BIOS and device firmware through fwupd and LVFS, plus your system updates. See [Updates](06-updates.md).

## Which laptops

It's made for the **Legion Pro 7 16IAX10H (Gen 10)**. Other recent Legions may work in part: the app only shows the
power modes and limits your laptop's firmware offers.

## What it can't do yet

The "LEGION" lettering on the lid can't be controlled yet. It isn't on the keyboard controller, the lighting WMI or
the Dynamic Lighting interface. For now only Legion Space on Windows can change it.
